# Funky Little Guys

A cute auto-battler you play in the browser. Build a deck, set your team up on a 4×4 board, and watch them brawl a rival squad.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any browser. It works on phones and on desktop, and there's nothing to install.

To play from a link, use GitHub Pages:
1. Go to **Settings → Pages**.
2. Set the source to **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Pick `main` and `/ (root)`, then save.

After about a minute the game is live at `https://ecwebdev.github.io/funkylittleguys/`.

## Play with a friend online

Tap **🌐 Play Online** on the main menu. One player taps **Host a game** and shares the 5-letter room code. The other player types the code in and taps **Join**. You each plan on your own device with your own deck, and the battle plays out live for both of you. It works straight from GitHub Pages, so there's no server to set up.

## How it works

- **Decks:** build up to 3 decks from the main menu. Each deck is 1 hero and 6 minis. The shop only offers minis from your active deck.
- **Gold:** every round you earn gold to buy minis. Buy a mini you already own to level it up, up to ★★★.
- **Board:** drag minis around your 4×4 board. Tanks go up front and ranged minis go in the back. The rival's side stays hidden until the fight starts.
- **Heroes:** your hero fills its energy bar by attacking, and a full bar fires its **Super**. Heroes unlock **Promotions** in rounds 3, 5 and 7.
- **Damage:** losing a round costs 1 HP, plus 1 HP for each enemy still standing. Both sides start with 20 HP.
